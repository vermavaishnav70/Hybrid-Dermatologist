Ablation Study — Diagnostic Analysis

• Removing Glued ML & DL (Concat): F1 improved by 0.0012 (0.1%). Simple concatenation instead of attention-weighted fusion.

• Removing Only ML (Classical Only): F1 dropped by 0.7173 (84.0%). CNN features zeroed out (Handcrafted ML features only).
